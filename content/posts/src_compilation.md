## General & Curriculums

- [OSSU: Computer Science](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science) – A complete self-taught education in Computer Science using online courses.
- [The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) – Essential tools (Shell, Vim, Git) they don't usually teach in school.
- [Roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/) – Step-by-step guides and paths to learning different technologies.

## Computer Science Fundamentals (Classics)

- [Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs (SICP)](https://web.mit.edu/6.001/6.037/sicp.pdf) – The legendary "Wizard Book" from MIT (6.001).
  - [Web Version](https://sarabander.github.io/sicp/) – An excellent, searchable HTML edition.
  - [Original 1986 Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLE18841CABEA24090) – Taught by authors Abelson and Sussman.
  - [2004 Course Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7BcsI5ueSNFPCEisbaoQ0kXIDX9rR5FF) – Taught by Prof. Eric Grimson.
- [Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective (CS:APP)](https://people.irisa.fr/Nicolas.Markey/PDF/Papers/BO16-CSAPP.pdf) – The definitive book on how systems execute programs, store information, and communicate.
  - [CS:APP Student Site & Labs](https://csapp.cs.cmu.edu/3e/students.html) – Official labs (Data Lab, Bomb Lab, etc.) for the CMU course.

## Operating Systems & Low-Level

- [OSTEP: Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) – A free, high-quality textbook on OS virtualization, concurrency, and persistence.
- [Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org/) – Build a modern computer system from the ground up (from logic gates to Tetris).
- [CPU.land](https://cpu.land) – A friendly guide to what happens when your computer starts up.
- [xv6: A Simple Unix-like Teaching OS](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.828/2017/xv6/book-rev10.pdf) – Official book for the MIT xv6 operating system.
- [OSDev Wiki](https://wiki.osdev.org/Expanded_Main_Page) – The primary resource for building your own operating system.
- [Modern Operating Systems (PDF)](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781292727899_A46720136/preview-9781292727899_A46720136.pdf) – A modern OS textbook.
- [Introduction to x86 Assembly](https://cs.lmu.edu/~ray/notes/nasmtutorial/) – Tutorial for NASM.
- [A Series on Linkers and Assembly](https://mcyoung.xyz/2021/11/29/assembly-1/) – High-quality deep dives into assembly, linker scripts, and target triples.
- [Memory Allocation Strategies](https://www.gingerbill.org/series/memory-allocation-strategies/) – In-depth series on custom memory allocators.
- [The Deadlock Empire](https://deadlockempire.github.io/) – An interactive game to learn about concurrency and race conditions.
- [The Little Book of Semaphores](https://greenteapress.com/wp/semaphores/) – A guide to multithreaded programming.

## Compilers & Programming Language Theory

- [Crafting Interpreters](https://craftinginterpreters.com/contents.html) – A brilliant guide to building a complete language from scratch (tree-walk and VM-based).
- [Build Your Own Lisp](https://buildyourownlisp.com/contents) – Learn C and build a Lisp dialect at the same time.

## Browser Internals & Web Engines

- [Browser Engineering](https://browser.engineering/) – Build a basic web browser from scratch.
- [How Browsers Work (Web.dev)](https://web.dev/articles/howbrowserswork) – Behind the scenes of modern web browsers.
- [Life of a Pixel](https://youtu.be/K2QHdgAKP-s) – Chrome’s rendering pipeline explained by Chromium engineers.
- [Inside a Web Browser (Chrome Blog)](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/inside-browser-part1) – A 4-part series on the architecture of modern browsers.
- [Life of a Script](https://youtu.be/veYjbF1rt5o) – How browsers execute JavaScript.
- [Chromium University](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL9ioqAuyl6ULp1f36EEjIN1vSBEfsb-0a) – Deep dives into Chromium's codebase and architecture.
- [How Browsers Work (Old but relevant)](https://www.taligarsiel.com/Projects/howbrowserswork1.htm) – Detailed technical breakdown of rendering engines.
- [How Web Works](https://github.com/vasanthk/how-web-works) – High-level overview with extensive reading links.
- [Toy Layout Engine](https://limpet.net/mbrubeck/2014/08/08/toy-layout-engine-1.html) – Building a basic browser engine in Rust.

## Networking & Protocols

- [Beej's Guide to Network Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/html/split/index.html) – The industry standard for learning socket programming.
- [High Performance Browser Networking](https://hpbn.co/) – A deep dive into TCP, UDP, TLS, and web protocols ([PDF Mirror](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/varunkashyapks/Books/refs/heads/master/High-Performance-Browser-Networking-Ilya-Grigorik.pdf)).
- [HTTP/3 Explained](https://http3-explained.haxx.se) & [HTTP/2 Explained](https://http2-explained.haxx.se) – Detailed guides from the creator of curl.
- [WebSocket Protocol Guide](https://websocket.org/guides/websocket-protocol/) – Understanding real-time web communication.

## Graphics & Shaders

- [Learn OpenGL](https://learnopengl.com) – The best resource for starting with modern OpenGL.
- [Scratchapixel](https://www.scratchapixel.com/) – Learning computer graphics from the ground up using math and code.
- [Shader Academy](https://shaderacademy.com/explore) – A LeetCode-like platform for practicing shaders.
- [Shader Tutorial](https://shader-tutorial.dev/) – A beginner-friendly guide to shaders.
- [The Book of Shaders](https://thebookofshaders.com/) – A gentle step-by-step guide through the abstract and complex universe of Shaders.
- [Computer Graphics Resources (Legends2k)](https://legends2k.github.io/note/cg_resources/) – A massive list of graphics links and notes.
- [Stanford CS248A: Computer Graphics](https://gfxcourses.stanford.edu/cs248a/winter24) – Course materials from Stanford's graphics program.
- [Graphics Programming Index](https://gist.github.com/notnotrobby/ceef71527b4f15869133ba7b397912e9) – A massive list of freely available resources to study computer graphics programming.

## Cybersecurity & Reverse Engineering

- [Cybersecurity Base (MOOC)](https://cybersecuritybase.mooc.fi/) – University of Helsinki’s course on security.
- [Linux Journey](https://linuxjourney.com/) – Learn the Linux command line and internals.
- [OverTheWire Wargames](https://overthewire.org/wargames/) – Practice security concepts through fun, tiered challenges.
- [Awesome Pentest](https://github.com/Muhammd/Awesome-Pentest) – Curated list of penetration testing resources.
- [Awesome Blue Team](https://github.com/fabacab/awesome-cybersecurity-blueteam) – Resources for defensive security and incident response.
- [Chrome Sandbox Escape](https://ading.dev/blog/posts/chrome_sandbox_escape.html) – An deep-dive technical blog on browser exploitation.
- [Picosnitch](https://github.com/elesiuta/picosnitch) – Monitor what your applications are doing on the network.

## AI, Machine Learning & Parallel Computing

- [Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/) – A free online book about the math behind AI.
- [The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/) – Visual guide to the architecture behind LLMs like GPT.
- [nn.labml.ai](https://nn.labml.ai/) – Annotated PyTorch implementations of complex neural network architectures.
- [ML Engineering Online Book](https://github.com/stas00/ml-engineering) – A comprehensive guide to the engineering side of machine learning.
- [Foundations of Computer Vision](https://visionbook.mit.edu/) – MIT’s foundational textbook on computer vision.
- [Zero to Mastery Learn PyTorch](https://www.learnpytorch.io/) – An end-to-end curriculum for learning PyTorch for deep learning.
- [Stanford CS 149: Parallel Computing](https://gfxcourses.stanford.edu/cs149/fall25/) – Course materials for high-performance parallel programming.
- [GPU Engineering](https://gpuengineering.com/) – A massive, curated collection of resources covering foundational books, frameworks (CUDA, ROCm, Triton), and multi-GPU systems.
- [LeetGPU](https://leetgpu.com/) – Practice and master GPU kernel programming.
- [Awesome GPU (Research)](https://github.com/Jokeren/Awesome-GPU) – A curated list of research papers and materials on GPU architecture and computing.
- [CUDA C Programming Guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-c-programming-guide/) – Official NVIDIA guide for GPU programming.
- [CUDA Programming Course (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5B692fm6--vWLhYPqLcEu6RF3hXjEyJr) – Video series on parallel computing.
- [Programming Massively Parallel Processors (PDF)](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~rijurekha/col851/necessary_reading/cudabook.pdf) – Academic textbook for CUDA.

## Software Architecture & Engineering Blogs

- [The Architecture of Open Source Applications (AOSA)](https://aosabook.org/en/index.html) – Learn how major software projects (Git, Nginx, etc.) are structured.
- [Ciechanowski’s Blog](https://ciechanow.ski/archives/) – Incredible interactive 3D visualizations of mechanical and engineering concepts.
- [RFleury.com](https://www.rfleury.com/) – Low-level systems programming and performance blog.
- [Clay](https://github.com/nicbarker/clay) – A high-performance UI layout library in C.

## Tools & Problem Solving

- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/) – Annual programming puzzles released every December.
- [Python Challenge](http://pythonchallenge.com) – A riddle-based website where you solve puzzles using Python code.
- [Grep.app](https://grep.app) – Search across half a million git repos.
- [Codebrowser.dev](https://codebrowser.dev/) – Browse large C/C++ projects (like the Linux kernel) with cross-references.
- [Toffeeshare](https://toffeeshare.com/) – Peer-to-peer file sharing via the browser.
- [0x0.st](https://0x0.st/) – A minimal, no-bullshit ephemeral file host and pastebin for the command line.
- [12ft Ladder](https://12ft.io/) – A tool to bypass simple web paywalls.
